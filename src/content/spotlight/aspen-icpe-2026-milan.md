The AsPEN Special Interest Group held its annual meeting during ICPE 2026, the annual meeting of the International Society for Pharmacoepidemiology (ISPE), in Milan, Italy, on September 1, 2026.

The meeting covered progress on AsPEN's international survey of pregnancy healthcare databases and on the AsPEN roadmap paper. Members also marked the official launch of the AsPEN website, which brings the network's databases, publications, activities, and member resources together in one place.

The group then discussed recent changes to ISPE policy and what they will mean for AsPEN's governance.

AsPEN thanks everyone who joined the meeting, and looks forward to continuing this work together.
